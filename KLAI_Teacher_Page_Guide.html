KLAI Teacher Page — How to Use It

A step-by-step guide for our native-speaking teachers

KLAI Language Academy (클라이 어학원) · Pyeongtaek · Version 2 — August 2026

⚠️ Please read this first — where we are right now

  • Student and parent accounts are not connected yet. Today, "Publish" only saves your record. Nothing is sent to a family. The words "published", "shared" and "shows on the parent page" describe how it will work once the student and parent pages are switched on.
  • For now there are only two jobs: (1) learn to record your class quickly and consistently, and (2) understand how those daily records become the end-of-month report.
  • This page is still being built and it will change. Buttons, labels, wording and some rules will be revised over the coming weeks. Several buttons already say "coming soon" on purpose, and there is a list of current quirks in section 2 — read it before you touch anything.
  • Your feedback changes the page. If something is confusing, slow, or missing, tell the director. That is exactly what this period is for.

1Before you start, and signing in

The page runs in a normal web browser. There is nothing to install.

  • Devices: laptop, iPad, iPhone or Android phone. Chrome, Safari and Edge all work.
  • Best experience: a laptop, or an iPad in landscape. The class grid is wide — on a phone you drag it sideways with your finger.
  • Language: the page opens in English for you. There is a 한국어 / English switch at the top left, and it remembers your choice on that device.
  • 1Teacher ID — type your full email addressIncluding the @ and everything after it. The grey example inside the box says "e.g. anna" — ignore it. A short name on its own will always be rejected.
  • 2PasswordPress Enter here and it signs you in — you don't have to reach for the button.
  • 3Sign in

If you see "ID or password is incorrect", the usual cause is a missing @domain or a trailing space. If it fails twice, stop and ask the director — repeated attempts can get the account temporarily blocked.

There is a Log out button on the top right. Use it on any shared or school computer.

The one habit that matters more than anything else: fill this in on the same day as the class, before you leave the building. A record written three days later is a guess, and a guess is worse than nothing on a report that goes to a parent.

3Your daily routine

Do this at the end of every lesson, in this order. About five minutes for a normal class, less once the order is familiar. The rest of this guide explains each step in detail — but this is the whole job.

  1. Set the date first, then your own name in the Teacher dropdown, then your class.
  2. Set the test — Vocab Test, Monthly Test or Level Test.
  3. Attendance: go down the column. Every row starts on "Present".
  4. Homework: tick All in the header, then untick whoever did not submit.
  5. Scores: type the number correct. The total is already filled in.
  6. Attitude: set every row one at a time. Every row starts on "Excellent".
  7. Notes and videos: only for the students who need one today. Most days that is nobody.
  8. Class note: one line — what you covered, the homework, and anything coming up. Press Publish.
  9. Teacher log: anything the director should know. Press 💾 Save.
  10. Check the Publish column, then press Save & publish class and wait for the confirmation.

4Set teacher, class and date

Everything you type is filed under four things: teacher + class + date + test. Set all four before you type anything else.

  • 1한국어 / EnglishSwitches the whole page, including the attendance and attitude words. It also reloads the grid — anything you have typed and not saved is lost. Choose your language at the start, not in the middle.
  • 2Teacher — leave this on your own nameThis dropdown, not your login, decides whose records you are reading and writing. It opens on the first teacher in the list for everyone, so check it every time. If your name is not in the list, stop and see the director — do not use a colleague's.
  • 3ClassYour timetable slots, e.g. 3:00 IB Y1 (21/22). Choosing one loads that roster into the grid below.
  • 4DateThe lesson date. It always opens on 18 August 2026 — set it by hand every time.
Before you change any of these four: press Save draft for the grid, Publish for the class note, and 💾 Save for the teacher log. All three, separately. Anything not saved by all three — including note text and video links you typed but did not save in the pop-up — is gone when the table reloads.

6Choosing the test

  • 1TestVocab Test · Monthly Test · Level Test. Pick the one that matches what you actually ran today.
  • 2+ New test Not built yetShows "coming soon". Use one of the three above.
  • 3Today · class note (shared) — the left-hand boxOne note for the whole class. See section 7.
  • 4Teacher log · work report (private) — the right-hand boxFor staff only. See section 7.

The test you choose sets the default total in the score column: Vocab Test → 6, Monthly Test → 25, Level Test → blank. You can overwrite the total on any individual row.

Why the test matters: a record is filed under teacher + class + date + test. So you can record a vocab quiz and a monthly test on the same day and neither erases the other. Choose the test before you start typing scores.

On a day with no test: there is no "no test" option yet, so the record still files under whichever test is showing. Just leave the left-hand score box empty. The total will still show 6 or 25 — ignore it.

7The two note boxes

They look alike and they do opposite things. The left-hand box is for families. The right-hand box is for staff.

📋 Today · class note (shared) — left-hand box

  • What it is: one note for the entire class — what you covered, the homework, any notice.
  • To post it: write it, then press Publish. The box locks and reads "✅ Published to students & parents".
  • To change it: press ✏️ Edit, fix the text, then press 🔄 Re-publish. If you press Edit and never press Re-publish, your change is not applied.
  • Example: Today: Unit 5 vocabulary · HW: workbook p.12 · Vocab test on Friday

🗒️ Teacher log · work report (private) — right-hand box

  • What it is: your record for the office — how the lesson went, handovers, anything the director should know.
  • To save it: write it, then press 💾 Save. It locks and reads "🔒 Private · saved". Press ✏️ Edit to change it.
  • Not shown to students or parents. But it is not private from other staff either — write it as you would write an email to the director.
  • Example: Class 3 finished Unit 5 · Jihoon needs a parent call · low textbook stock — report to director

Both boxes are saved per class and per date. Change the date at the top and you are looking at that day's note and that day's log — which is how you re-read last week before a parent call.

Keep a child's family circumstances, health or emotional state out of the teacher log. Those belong in a 🔒 Sensitive individual note (section 9), or better, in a conversation with the director.

8The class grid, column by column

The whole class on one screen, like a spreadsheet. The app calls this section "Scores & Attendance".

  • 1AttendancePresent · Late · Absent · Excused. "Excused" means the family told us in advance. Every row starts on "Present" — a row you never touch is recorded as present.
  • 2HomeworkTicked = submitted. The All box in the header ticks the whole column at once. Every row starts unticked.
  • 3Score (correct / total)Left box = number correct, right box = total questions. Leave the left box empty if there was no test. Empty means "no test". Zero means "sat the test and scored nothing". They are not the same.
  • 4AttitudeExcellent · Good · Needs work. Every row starts on "Excellent", so set every student one at a time — otherwise you are giving a positive evaluation nobody actually made.
  • 5Video🎬＋ opens a pop-up to attach a clip. Once attached the icon becomes 🎬✓. See section 9.
  • 6Note 🔒📝 opens the individual note pop-up. Once written, the icon shows the type: 📝• general, ⚠️• repeated, 🔒• sensitive. See section 9.
  • 7PublishTicked = this student's row is included when you press "Save & publish class". Every row starts ticked. The header has an All box to untick the whole column at once.

On a phone, drag the grid sideways to reach the right-hand columns. Nothing is hidden — it just does not fit a narrow screen.

9Individual notes and videos

📝 The individual note, and the three note types

This is the highest-stakes part of the page. It exists to protect the child, the family and you — it is the one section worth reading twice.

  • 1Note typeThree choices — the table below explains each.
  • 2The rule boxChanges with the type and states exactly what that type does. The rules differ, so it is worth a glance.
  • 3The note itselfObjective facts only — see the writing rule below.
  • 4Share with parentOff by default — you have to tick it. It cannot be ticked at all for a Sensitive note.
  • 5SaveThe note is only kept when you press Save. Cancel throws it away, and so does closing the page.
The three note types
TypeUse it forWhat the system does
📝 General
(internal)		Everyday observations, and your running tally of how many times you have reminded a student about something.Not shared unless you tick the box. This is where you build the evidence before anything is escalated.
⚠️ Repeated 3×+
(shared · affects monthly)		Only an issue that has not changed after you have reminded the student three or more times.Recommended for sharing with the parent. When shared, it feeds the attitude score on the monthly report.
🔒 Sensitive
(never shared)		Family circumstances, emotional state, health — anything a parent should hear from a person, not read in an app.The share tick box is switched off and you cannot turn it on. Staff only. Never on the monthly report.

There is no automatic counter. You keep the tally inside the note text, with the dates, every time — e.g. "HW not submitted — 2nd time (Aug 4, 11). Reminded." When your own tally reaches three, change the type to ⚠️ Repeated 3×+.

The escalation path — one way, and deliberate

  1. Log it as 📝 General each time it happens, with the date and the running count.
  2. When the count reaches three and nothing has changed, change the type to ⚠️ Repeated 3×+.
  3. Tell the director or your Korean co-teacher before you tick "Share with parent". The family should hear it from a person; the written note confirms a conversation, it does not start one.
  4. When the behaviour improves, say so — write a new General note recording the improvement, so the month does not end on the worst week.
How to write a note. Record what happened, how often, and what you did about it — never a judgement about the child.
Write this: "Homework not submitted 3 times (Aug 4, 11, 18). Reminded each time."
Not this: "Lazy, doesn't care."
The first is a fact a parent can act on. The second is an opinion we cannot defend.
Anything about a family situation, a child's mental health, or a safeguarding worry: mark it 🔒 Sensitive and speak to the director in person the same day. The app is not the right way to deliver that news.

🎬 Attaching a video

Consent first, camera second. Before filming, confirm with the director that we hold written parental consent for that child. Film one child at a time — a clip showing classmates needs consent from every family in it. Upload to YouTube as Unlisted (never Public) or to a restricted Drive folder, paste the link here, then delete the clip from your phone.
  • 1Paste the linkThere is no upload button here — the field takes a link only. Upload to YouTube (Unlisted) or Drive first.
  • 2Show to student & parentThis one arrives ticked. Untick it until the director confirms the consent is on file.
  • 3SaveCancel discards the link.

11Publish and Save

  • 1Save draftSaves the grid only — not the class note and not the teacher log, which have their own buttons. It also marks every row as not published. So use it before you have published, not after: if you already published this class today, Save draft will un-publish it.
  • 2Save & publish classSaves the grid, and marks as published only the rows whose Publish box is ticked.

A short message appears at the bottom of the screen to confirm. It still contains the word "(demo)" — that is leftover wording, not a sign that nothing was saved. Only a message starting "Save failed:" means the save did not go through; if you see one, do not close the tab — check your connection and press the button again.

Two things worth knowing

  • Publish starts ticked for every student. Scan the last column before you press "Save & publish class".
  • One record per teacher + class + date + test + student. Saving the same combination again overwrites the earlier save — which is what lets you correct a mistake, and also why the date must be right before you type.
How do I fix something I already saved?

Set the teacher, class, date and test back to exactly the combination you saved under. The grid reloads with what you saved. Change what is wrong and press the same Save button again — the corrected version replaces the old one.

Two cautions. First, there is no history and no undo: the old version is gone, so get it right the second time. Second, if the grid comes back empty when you expected records, do not save — that usually means the records failed to load rather than that they do not exist, and saving would overwrite good data with blanks. Tell the director instead.

12How this becomes the monthly report

The report will not be something you write at the end of the month. It will be assembled from what you recorded every lesson.

When the report tool is built, each student's report will be assembled from the following — which is exactly why these five things are the ones worth recording carefully now:

Where each part of the monthly report will come from
Part of the reportComes from
AttendanceThe month's pattern of Present / Late / Absent / Excused entries.
HomeworkThe submitted rate, from the homework tick.
ScoresThe Vocab Test entries across the month, plus the Monthly Test at the end.
AttitudeThe Excellent / Good / Needs work column, plus any note marked ⚠️ Repeated 3×+ and shared.
What the class coveredYour daily class notes.

The Reading-class Report button that will generate and publish it is not built yet. That is the next piece of work.

What this means for you in practice: record every class as you go and the report will assemble itself — you will only need to check it. Skip days and there is nothing to assemble. That is the entire reason we are practising the recording habit now, before the report tool exists.

At the end of the month

  1. Run your monthly test and enter it with the test set to Monthly Test (total 25 by default).
  2. For any student with a shared ⚠️ Repeated note, re-read it and check it is still factual and still true.
  3. Tell the director the class is ready.
Do not re-open old dates and press Save to "catch up" on days you missed. A row you have not touched saves as Present / Excellent / Publish-ON, so catching up invents attendance that never happened. If a day is genuinely missing, tell the director and leave it missing. A gap is honest; an invented record is not.

13Situations you will meet

A student stops coming

Keep marking them Absent every lesson — or Excused if the family told us in advance. Do not skip the row and do not leave them on the "Present" default.

After three consecutive absences, tell the director the same day. The Korean staff, not you, contact the family. Note the date you reported it in your teacher log.

A student is in your class but not in the list

New, trial and recently-moved students cannot be recorded yet — rosters are added by hand at the moment. Keep them on paper and tell the director.

In your first week, also check the list against your own register. Some English spellings and codes are still unverified.

A parent asks you about their child directly

At the door, by phone, or on KakaoTalk — be warm, be brief, and hand it over: "Let me ask the office to call you so nothing gets lost in translation."

Never discuss another child, never discuss level placement, fees or a move to another class, and never promise an outcome. Write down what they asked in your teacher log the same day and tell the director.

You realise you recorded the wrong class or the wrong date

Go back to the wrong combination, clear what you entered there, and save it — then enter it again under the right one. Tell the director if a published record was involved, because there is no history to check against.

Something else goes wrong

Screenshot it and send it to the director with the class, the date and roughly what you were doing. That is genuinely useful — this page is being built around what you report during exactly this period.

14Do / Don't

✅ Do

  • Record on the day of the lesson.
  • Set the date, and check the Teacher dropdown is on your name, before typing anything.
  • Save before changing teacher, class, date, test or language.
  • Set Attendance and Attitude on every row, one at a time.
  • Write notes as dated facts with a count.
  • Use General notes to build up to a Repeated one.
  • Mark anything delicate as Sensitive and speak to the director.
  • Press Save inside the note and video pop-ups.
  • Tell the director what is awkward about this page.

🚫 Don't

  • Don't leave Attitude untouched and assume it is blank — it is "Excellent".
  • Don't press "Save & publish class" without scanning the Publish column.
  • Don't press "Save draft" on a class you already published — it un-publishes it.
  • Don't back-fill missed days from memory.
  • Don't write opinions or labels about a child.
  • Don't put family, health or emotional matters in a shared note or in the teacher log.
  • Don't film a student before checking consent with the director.
  • Don't work under a colleague's name in the Teacher dropdown.
  • Don't share your login.

📌 The three things to remember

  1. No family sees any of this yet. We are building the habit, not publishing to parents.
  2. Right now there are only two jobs: record every lesson on the day, and understand how those records become the monthly report.
  3. The page will change. Tell the director what gets in your way — that is how it gets better.

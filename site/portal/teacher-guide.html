Your Teacher Page
Quick Guide

Attendance · reports · make-ups — all in one place

🔑 GETTING IN
🌐
Go to amyschool.kr

Tap Log in → choose Teacher. Use the ID & password you were given. (Chrome or Safari)

🇰🇷
Language toggle 한국어 / EN

Top-left switch flips the whole page between Korean and English. Pick EN — your choice is remembered.

📅
Class & date pickers

Choose your class and the date at the top. The student list and records load for that slot.

📝 DAILY CLASS RECORDS
✅
Attendance

Set Present / Late / Absent / Make-up for each student.

📚
Homework · Progress · Result

Tap the homework button (Done / Not done / No HW), then type the book, unit·page and result.

🗒️
Notes late/absent reason

Write the reason for a late/absent here — it shows on the director's dashboard.

💾
Save

Tap Save (or press Enter in a field). Records sync live to the director.

🎬 PER-STUDENT EXTRAS
🎥
Class video

Add a lesson-video link per student. If made public, parents can watch it.

🔒
Private memo

A note only the teacher & director see. Turn public off to hide a student's record from parents.

📊
Score entry

Enter correct / total for the day's test.

🧰 TEACHER TOOLS
🗣️
Zoom & Keystone

Elia English · EQ Talk · Group Talk.

📚
IB PPT & TG

Lesson slides & teaching guides.

🐾
Pathways (3rd Ed.)

NGL teaching tools.

📒
Resources (Notion)

Find class materials & forms.

✍️
Writing Lab

Post topics · worksheets · 1:1 feedback.

🗒️ TEACHER LOG & MAKE-UP
📓
Teacher log (private)

Your private daily notes / work report — not shown to students or parents. Change the date to see past logs.

🔁
Today's make-up

Students from your class with a make-up today appear automatically (name + time). Shows in English too.

📦 ORDERING BOOKS
📦
Book Order button (top-right)

Tap it — a new window opens. Add one or more classes, pick the book, quantity and a note, then Submit. The desk gets it and orders the books; you'll see ✅ when done.

📦 Open Book Order
💡 TIPS
• Add the site to your home screen to open it like an app (Safari share → “Add to Home Screen”).
• Everything saves to the cloud — safe to close and reopen, even on another device.
• Press Enter in a field to save quickly.
